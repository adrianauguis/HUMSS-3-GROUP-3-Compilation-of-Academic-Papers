Reaction Paper: The Apple

“An Award Winning Tale of a Young Boy Realising the Importance of Food” · Hindi short movie

Introduction and General Impression

The short film “The Apple” tells the story of two young boys whose different circumstances reveal the inequality that exists in everyday life. One child has access to food and the comforts of childhood, while the other struggles to meet basic needs. Their lives connect through the sharing and exchange of an apple, showing how something ordinary to one person can be deeply valuable to another.

Our group felt sadness while watching the film because it showed that not all children have the same opportunities. It reminded us that food should never be taken for granted and that even a small act of kindness can make a meaningful difference in another person’s life.

Reflection and Personal Response

The strongest message for us was the contrast between the two boys. Neither child chose the family or economic situation into which he was born, yet their daily experiences are very different. One can enjoy meals without worrying about the next day, while the other has to struggle for something as basic as food. This made us reflect on how unfair life can be when access to basic needs depends on circumstances beyond a child’s control.

The apple serves as a powerful symbol. For a child who has plenty, it may be just an ordinary snack; for a hungry child, it can be precious because it helps satisfy an empty stomach. The film taught us that value is not determined only by price, but also by how much something is needed. It encouraged us to appreciate our meals, our families, and the privileges we may overlook, and to avoid wasting food simply because we do not like it.

The film also shows that poverty is more than hunger. It can limit a child’s chance to attend school, play, and enjoy childhood. At the same time, people experiencing poverty deserve dignity and respect, not only temporary pity. The story encouraged us to be more thoughtful toward others and to recognize that sharing what we have can help someone facing difficult circumstances.

Conclusion

Overall, “The Apple” reminded our group to be grateful for the food and opportunities we have, to avoid food waste, and to share whenever possible. It taught us that everyone deserves enough food and to be treated with kindness and respect. Even a simple gesture, such as sharing an apple, can carry a powerful message and improve someone’s day or life.
